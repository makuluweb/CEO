What to clock — not the content
  • What they foreground first. The opening clause is the unfiltered priority.
  • The pronoun. “I” leans Mastery / Order · “we” leans Belonging · “you just figure it out” leans Freedom.
  • The verb. built, ran, protected → Order · brought together, helped → Belonging · won, beat, drove → Mastery · explored, learned, questioned → Freedom.

One answer is a hypothesis — carry two or three; the next confirms or breaks the first. Polished people perform the proud question, so the friction question is your tiebreaker.
